# Retrieval Impact Notes

## Cases Where Retrieval Helped

### 1. What is a Backup?
The system retrieved `backup_manager_overview.txt` with a relatively high similarity score of 0.6427, so the retrieved context was relevant to the question, although the generated answer was incomplete.

### 2. What is storage?
The system retrieved `storage_configuration.txt` with a similarity score of 0.5149, which was directly related to storage and provided useful context for answering the question.

### 3. What is a Password?
The system retrieved `authentication.txt` with a similarity score of 0.2976, which was relevant because authentication information is related to passwords and credentials.

## Cases Where Retrieval Did Not Help

### 1. What is Wi-Fi?
The system retrieved `backup_manager_overview.txt` with a low similarity score of 0.2451, which was unrelated to Wi-Fi and did not provide useful information for the answer.

### 2. What is Phishing?
The system retrieved `backup_manager_overview.txt` with a low similarity score of 0.1848, showing that the knowledge base did not contain relevant information about phishing.

### 3. What is QuantumOS?
The system retrieved `backup_manager_overview.txt` with a very low similarity score of 0.1538, which was unrelated to QuantumOS and therefore did not help answer the question.

## Overall Observation

Retrieval works better when the question is related to the information available in the knowledge base. For questions outside the knowledge base, the system may retrieve unrelated backup documents. The evaluation also shows that retrieval quality and answer-generation quality are separate issues: relevant context can be retrieved while the generated answer may still be incomplete.
