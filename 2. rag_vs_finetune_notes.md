## 5 RAG Failure Reasons
1. Wrong chunk retrieved — The system may retrieve information that is related to the question but doesn't actually contain the answer.
2. Relevant chunk not retrieved — The correct information may exist in the knowledge base, but the retrieval system may fail to find it.
3. Conflicting information — Two retrieved documents may provide different or contradictory information.
4. Outdated information — The retrieved source may contain old instructions that are no longer correct.
5. Too much irrelevant context — Large or poorly divided chunks can include unnecessary information, making it harder for the model to focus on the useful information.
